Application Overview

This Privacy Policy applies to the mobile application Pomora: Focus Timer ("the Application", "the App", "we", "us", or "our").

  • Application Name: Pomora: Focus Timer
  • Application Category: Productivity / Focus Timer / Pomodoro Technique
  • Primary Purpose: Helping users manage work/study intervals, boost concentration through the Pomodoro technique, and maximize daily productivity.
  • Last Updated: August 8, 2026
02

Introduction

We respect your fundamental right to privacy. We created Pomora: Focus Timer with a core philosophy: a productivity application should help you focus without intruding into your personal life or monitoring your digital habits.

This Privacy Policy clearly details our data practices, confirming our commitment to protecting your privacy when using our application. By installing and using Pomora: Focus Timer, you can enjoy a completely private and standalone Pomodoro experience.

Privacy Guarantee: Pomora does not collect, record, track, sell, or transmit any of your personal information.
03

Information We Collect

We explicitly state that Pomora: Focus Timer does NOT collect any personal data.

  • No Personal Identifiable Information (PII): We do not collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, IP addresses, location data, or device identifiers.
  • No User Account System: The application operates without user registration, social login, or password creation. You can use all features immediately upon installation without providing any credentials.
  • Local Application Preferences Only: The app may store non-personal settings locally on your device (such as your chosen timer durations, sound preferences, theme colors, or total completed focus sessions). This data is strictly non-identifiable and stays exclusively on your physical device.
04

How We Use Information

Because we do not gather personal information, we do not use your data for commercial, marketing, or analytical purposes. The locally saved configurations on your device are utilized strictly for:

  • Providing Core Application Features: Remembering your custom focus intervals, short breaks, long break durations, and preferred alert sounds.
  • Local Notifications & Reminders: Operating local device notifications to alert you when a focus session finishes or when a break starts. Local notifications execute entirely on your operating system without external servers.
  • Session Statistics: Displaying your personal focus progress locally on your screen during active usage.
05

Data Storage & Transfer

100% On-Device Local Storage: All data generated while using Pomora (such as timer configurations and session tallies) is stored exclusively within your device's native local storage (e.g., SharedPreferences on Android).

Zero Server Transmission: We do not host remote servers or cloud databases for Pomora. Your data is never transmitted over the Internet, backed up to external servers by us, or shared across external networks.

Data Deletion: Since all data resides locally on your device, uninstalling the Pomora application will permanently remove all associated local preferences and settings from your device immediately.

06

Third Party Services

To preserve maximum privacy, speed, and battery efficiency, Pomora does not integrate any third-party SDKs or external services.

  • No Advertising SDKs: No third-party ad networks (such as AdMob or Unity Ads) are included in the application.
  • No Analytics or Tracking Tools: No telemetry tools (such as Google Analytics for Firebase, Mixpanel, or Flurry) are utilized to track your app usage.
  • No Third-Party Data Sharing: We do not share, sell, trade, or rent any data with third parties, marketing partners, or data brokers.
07

Children's Privacy

Pomora: Focus Timer is a general audience productivity utility designed to help anyone improve concentration and time management.

The Application is not directed specifically to children under the age of 13 (or under 16 in certain jurisdictions). Because we do not collect any personal information whatsoever, we do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us at the email address below so we can address your inquiry immediately.

08

Data Security

The security of your privacy is built into our application architecture by design:

  • By storing all application data locally on your device and abstaining from remote cloud servers, we eliminate remote database breach risks.
  • Your local app data is protected by your mobile device's built-in operating system security sandbox.

We recommend keeping your smartphone operating system updated to ensure the continuous security of your device environment.

Any updates will be published directly on this page with an updated "Effective Date" at the top of the document. We encourage you to review this Privacy Policy periodically to stay informed about how we maintain your privacy.
